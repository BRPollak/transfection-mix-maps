TRANSFECTION MIX MAPS 1.0.1
Apple Silicon · macOS 14 or later

Create LT1 or L2000 Excel mix maps from plate CSVs and DNA concentrations
in Google Sheets. Python and all dependencies are included.

RELEASE STATUS
Version 1.0.1 is currently a source update. No v1.0.1 installer has been built
or published. The available published installer remains v1.0.0; see README.md
for its download link and BUILDING.md to run v1.0.1 from source. The installation
steps below apply when a v1.0.1 installer is built.

INSTALL OR UPDATE
1. Quit Transfection Mix Maps if it is running.
2. Open Transfection-Mix-Maps-1.0.1-arm64.pkg and follow the Installer prompts.
3. Open Transfection Mix Maps from Applications.

The installer replaces the app in /Applications and preserves your Google
connection, cached concentrations, and preferences. No uninstall is needed.
Keep the small launcher window open while working in your browser. Quit
through that window when finished.

USE THE APP
1. Expand One-time Google setup and follow its Desktop OAuth instructions.
   Save the client JSON file, sign in, and enter your Google Sheet URL or ID.
2. Press Confirm Sheet & refresh concentrations. The Sheet needs Plasmid and
   Concentration (ng/uL) columns. Refresh it after changing stock values.
3. Select Plate type, then choose up to five plate CSVs. The plate type applies
   to the whole batch. Select LT1 or L2000 and review the settings.
4. Choose a save folder. One plate uses its CSV's folder automatically;
   multiple plates require a folder selection with Save Excel files to….
5. Press Generate to prepare and review the maps. This does not save files.
6. Press Download for each workbook to save it to the selected folder.
   You can change that folder after generating. Existing files are preserved.

L2000 supports up to five positive total DNA masses per plate. Prepare each
bulk recipe separately and follow its assigned wells and aliquot volume.
Symbols ▲ ● ■ ◆ ★ identify multiple mixes in the preview and workbook.
Preparation includes overage; deliver only the configured final volume.

PLATE TYPES
96-well: A1-H12 (8 rows x 12 columns)
48-well: A1-F8 (6 rows x 8 columns)
24-well: A1-D6 (4 rows x 6 columns)
12-well: A1-C4 (3 rows x 4 columns)
6-well: A1-B3 (2 rows x 3 columns)
Single well (dish): A1

Plate type starts at 48-well each new session and is not saved in preferences.
Changing it retains CSVs, rechecks them, and requires generating again. Wide
and long CSV formats remain supported. Unused unsupported wells with blank or
valid-zero DNA are ignored. Positive DNA outside the selected layout blocks
the batch; the error identifies its filename, CSV row, well, and allowed range.

The preview shows the complete plate. For printing, 96-well workbooks have
Mix Map (A-D) and Mix Map (E-H) tabs, each on one landscape Letter page.
Both repeat the whole-plate recipes: prepare each bulk recipe once, then use
its aliquots across both halves. Other plate types have one Mix Map tab.

The app remembers your Google connection and preferences on this Mac.
Select your plate CSVs again when you return. Private settings are stored at:
~/Library/Application Support/Transfection Mix Maps/local_state/

MACOS SECURITY
The installer is unsigned. The app uses an ad hoc signature and is not
Developer ID signed or notarized. If macOS blocks it, follow Apple's
instructions for opening an app you trust:
https://support.apple.com/102445

HELP
For input examples and full setup instructions, see README.md. In Finder,
choose Show Package Contents on the app, then open Contents > Resources.
VALIDATION.md describes the verification performed.
